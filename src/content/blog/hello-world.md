## Why a blog?

> Yoyo's little corner of the internet came into being.

You may call me Yoyo.

As for why I decided to start a blog, I suppose it has something to do with the human need to see and to be seen. To leave a trace of one's passing through this world, however faint it may be. To gather the scattered thoughts, fleeting moments, and half-formed ideas that might otherwise dissolve into the ordinary passage of time.
